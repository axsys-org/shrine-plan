# community

Community Starter, first cut (2026-11-18). Plan: `shrine-harness/note/networking-finish.md`, steps M, Q, R and
section 8.

- `scope.grove`: the general shapes. `item` (a name and what it says), `word` (something one world says to
  another, with its context, its subject and what it answers), and the shapes that extend them: `entry`,
  `invite`, `join`, `verdict`, `admit`, `introduce`. They sit in this mount until a Grove root can import
  another root's declarations; then they move to the scope mount.
- `main.grove`: `announcement`, `project`, `document`, the views of an item (summary, detail, edit) and one
  workspace: an outline of the community's tree beside a reader, seeded as a small society.
- `mount.foil`: the door at `/community` and the install of the instance under `/<world>/app/community`.

Not here yet: creating records from a pane, sending a request to another world, the catalog read by the Mesa
foot, invitations that work. Those are steps E, M and N.
