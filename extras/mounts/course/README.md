# course

A course as a template, and the first composition whose networking is read off a table.

`course.grove` declares five norms. Each is one row: where a thing sits, what it is, and in its metadata who
writes it (`by`) and who reads it (`for`). The mount's activation makes the course at `/<self>/app/course`,
writes the table beneath it from those norms (`weave/unit`, `weave/tabled`), and tells the scope foot the
course is this world's to keep. From then on:

- a seat that holds an invitation's number has its invitation minted, and is filled when its invitee is heard
  (`scope.foil`);
- whoever fills a seat is who the table means by it, and each world writes into the others' roots exactly what
  the table says (`weave.foil`).

Nothing here says what an assignment or a mark is, or who may see one, except the table.
