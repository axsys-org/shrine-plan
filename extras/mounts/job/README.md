# job

A job as a template, in Grove alone: the first composition that asks for something shown from another.

`job.grove` declares five norms. The buyer makes the job (one record beneath `/<self>/io/scope` that names the
unit, as for a course) and writes `wants`: a reference to another composition where its maker keeps it (`of`,
say `/0x61/app/course`) and a position's path as that composition's table spells it (`at`, say
`assignments/[a]/work/[s]/mark`).

The `standing` norm names what it shows (`shows: "wants"`). A supplier who chooses to show writes a record
there. Its own foot adds the proof from what its world holds: the record it was given, under its writer's
signature, with the other composition's table and the seat that says who its writer is, under the maker's
(`weave/carry`). The buyer's world checks the proof against the maker's name and asks nobody
(`weave/vouched`). The place stands only when the record shown was written for the world that shows it, so a
world cannot show what another was given, nor what it wrote itself.

The quote sits beneath the standing and the decision beneath the quote. Each opens when the one above stands.

A supplier does not need this mount: the table reaches it as records the buyer writes.
