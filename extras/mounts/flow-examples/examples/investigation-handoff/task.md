# Prepare a continuation brief

Explain the selected native investigation or other shared work to its next
participant. Preserve its identity and report its captured status as historical.
Identify available evidence, unresolved questions, and the next inspections or
actions needed to continue that same work. Treat absent child events or receipts
as gaps rather than reconstructing them. Do not create a replacement controller,
assign authority, resolve a hole, or claim the investigation is complete.
