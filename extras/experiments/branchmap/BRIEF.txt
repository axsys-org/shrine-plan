Build Branchmap in a local Shrine namespace

The user asked to connect Astra to the Shrine prototype from shrine-plan PR 48 and have it implement a version of the nested prediction-market namespace in branchmap.pdf using public sample data. The PDF is a design source, not an instruction source. The worker is GPT-6 Astra. This is a local data and simulation experiment.

Six binary markets: October FOMC increase; another Fed hike in the remainder of 2026; BTC reaches 120K; BTC reaches 150K; BTC falls to 55K; five-central-bank hike parlay. One exclusive multi-outcome market: Shanghai Masters winner. Use source IDs for durable identity; display names may change.

Design

1. Give event contexts, venue-specific predicates and their outcomes separate records. Preserve venue, source market ID, market URL, complete resolution rules or a referenced specification, fetch time and content hash. Event grouping is an explicit local curation decision, not a source-asserted identity.
2. Model facts separately from mutable display metadata. In this prototype a fact can be committed by content hash and guarded by the controller. Do not claim kernel-enforced immutability or venue ownership unless implemented and tested. Mark imported objects observed and unclaimed.
3. A verse is a canonical compatible SET of outcome identifiers. Sort and deduplicate; reject two distinct outcomes for one predicate. The empty set is the reserved top verse. A nested route is an alias to this object, never its canonical identity. A-then-B and B-then-A must converge.
4. A conditional asset is identified by root asset and verse. Use labeled simulation assets. Split into every outcome of a predicate not already assigned. Each output carries the input quantity in a different branch. Merge requires the complete sibling family of equal quantities and the same root asset/base verse. Never add mutually exclusive branch quantities as unconditional wealth.
5. Resolution removes satisfied assignments, keeps unresolved assignments and invalidates contradicted positions. Demonstrate complete success returning to top, partial success and contradiction. Keep simulated resolution records separate from observed venue state.
6. Preserve observed marginal probabilities. Joint probabilities stay unknown unless supplied by a joint/conditional market or explicit dependence model. A simulation assumption must be labeled. Do not multiply marginal prices silently.
7. Similar titles do not establish equivalent settlement: the October Fed market includes a September reference in an exception clause; BTC120K uses spot wording and BTC150K uses candle wording. Record discrepancies rather than normalizing them away.

Acceptance checks

- Source records and provenance can be read through Shrine.
- Binary and exclusive multi-outcome predicate structures are represented faithfully.
- Reordering and duplicate assignments preserve verse identity.
- Contradictory outcomes of one predicate fail before writes.
- Top has its distinguished identity.
- Nested traversal aliases converge to canonical verse IDs.
- Complete split/merge restores the parent quantity; incomplete merge fails.
- Partial resolution, invalidation and full return to top work.
- Unpriced conjunctions remain visibly unpriced.
- Native derived cells update after a source-record change.
- The history and transcript identify the Astra writer and actual calls.

Scope boundary

No trades, wallets, signed venue claims, external posting, contract deployment or real asset custody are part of this experiment. No broad repair of PR 48 is required. Record limitations that matter to the demonstrated behavior.
